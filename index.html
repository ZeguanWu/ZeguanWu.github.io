Short Bio

Zeguan Wu is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Pittsburgh. At Pitt, he develops continuous-variable and bosonic quantum algorithms for differential equations, scientific computing, and optimization, collaborating with Juan José Mendoza-Arenas, Peyman Givi, and Junyu Liu.

His research focuses on quantum optimization and numerical linear algebra, including quantum interior point methods, and continuous-variable quantum algorithms for scientific computing. He also develops AI-assisted mathematical research workflows that combine language models, Lean 4, numerical validation, and structured review for quantum algorithms and optimization.

He received his Ph.D. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from Lehigh University in 2025, advised by Tamás Terlaky and Xiu Yang. His dissertation is titled “Quantum Computing and Quantum Interior Point Methods.” He also held research appointments at Los Alamos National Laboratory and Pacific Northwest National Laboratory.
