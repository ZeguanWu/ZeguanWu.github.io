Short Bio

Zeguan Wu is a NASA Postdoc Fellow in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Pittsburgh. He is working with Juan Jose Mendoza Arenas, Peymanon Givi, and Junyu Liu on quantum computing algorithms in fluid dynamics. Before joining the University of Pittsburgh, he obtained his Ph.D. from Lehigh University under the supervision of Tamás Terlaky and Xiu Yang, where he worked on quantum linear algebra and quantum optimization algorithms. His research interests include quantum computing, artificial intelligence, optimization, and their intersections.
